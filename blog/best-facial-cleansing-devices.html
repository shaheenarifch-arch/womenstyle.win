Quick answer

The best facial cleansing device is the one that matches how you actually wash your face. For most people the standard LUNA 4 covers daily cleansing plus a firming massage; the LUNA 4 go is the budget and travel pick; the LUNA 4 mini suits a quick, dual-sided cleanse; and the LUNA 5 LED or LUNA 4 plus make sense only if you want light-based features on top of cleansing.

  • Soft, non-porous silicone touchpoints rather than nylon bristles
  • Short, timed sessions so you cleanse thoroughly without over-scrubbing
  • Battery life that fits your routine and travel habits
  • Whether the model needs a companion app to unlock settings
  • Extra features (massage, LED, near-infrared) you will really use

How we chose

No hands-on testing was performed for this guide. The article is based on published product information, retailer descriptions, available specifications and practical buying criteria.

We compared the facial cleansing devices currently featured on WomenStyle by size, session features, battery claims, app requirements and listed price, then matched each to a common routine: travel, quick daily cleansing, all-round use, LED interest or a firming focus. We did not rank by a single universal score, because the right device depends on how you wash your face.

How to choose

  1. Start with your routine. If you mainly need a fast, thorough cleanse, a compact model like the LUNA 4 go or LUNA 4 mini covers it; paying for massage or light modes only makes sense if you will use them.
  2. Match the skin-type version. Full-size LUNA 4 models come in versions for different skin types; picking the right one affects how the touchpoints feel on your skin.
  3. Check app requirements. Some models need the FOREO For You app to activate and customise settings; if you prefer a device that works straight out of the box, confirm this before you buy.
  4. Think about travel and charging. Battery claims vary by model; a long-lasting compact device is easier to pack than a full-size one.
  5. Pair it with a gentle cleanser. A non-gritty face wash, such as the Micro-Foam Cleanser 2.0 (100 ml), suits silicone devices better than scrubs.
  6. Review practical details: confirm the current price, delivery time, warranty and return policy on the retailer's page, since these can change.

Common buying mistakes

  • Paying for features you will not use. LED and firming modes add cost; if you only want a cleaner face, a simpler model is enough.
  • Ignoring the skin-type version. Choose the version that matches your skin rather than the first colour you like.
  • Overusing the device. Longer or more frequent sessions are not better; follow the timed routine and ease off if skin feels irritated.
  • Using a scrub with the device. Gritty cleansers add unnecessary friction; use a gentle face wash.
  • Not checking the app. Some models are hard to use without the app, which can frustrate readers who want a simple gadget.
  • Skipping the return policy. Confirm return terms before buying in case the device does not suit your skin.

Limitations and checks

Battery life, session times, light modes and app features are manufacturer claims, not results we measured. Specifications can change between product versions, so confirm details on the current listing before you buy.

This article cannot confirm current stock, exact pricing, shipping times or warranty terms, which are set by the retailer. It also cannot predict how any device will feel on your skin. If you have a skin condition, are using prescription skincare or notice ongoing irritation, ask a board-certified dermatologist before adding a cleansing device to your routine.

Frequently asked questions

Do facial cleansing devices clean better than hands?

A device adds consistent pulsation or massage, and some readers find that helps them cleanse for the full recommended time. Dermatology guidance still centres on a gentle cleanser, lukewarm water and no harsh scrubbing, so a device is an optional tool rather than a requirement for clean skin.

Are silicone cleansing devices better than bristle brushes?

Silicone touchpoints are non-porous and quick-drying, which is why brands describe them as more hygienic and easier to rinse than nylon bristles. They are also generally softer on the skin, but how your own skin responds still depends on pressure, frequency and the cleanser you use.

How often should I use a facial cleansing device?

Follow the routine on the product listing; the LUNA devices are designed for short sessions, about a minute for most models and up to two minutes on the LUNA 5 LED. If your skin feels tight, red or irritated, use it less often or stop.

Which LUNA is best for travel?

The LUNA 4 go is the smallest and lowest-priced device in this guide, and its listing describes up to 300 uses per full charge, which makes it the practical pick for a gym bag or carry-on.

Do I need the FOREO app?

Several current LUNA models connect to the FOREO For You app to activate the device and customise cleansing and massage settings. Check the listing for the model you want, and expect the app-connected models to be harder to use without it.

What does the LED on the LUNA 5 LED do?

According to retailer listings, the LUNA 5 LED pairs T-Sonic cleansing with red, blue and near-infrared light modes. LED features are cosmetic and results vary; they are not a medical treatment.

Is the LUNA 4 plus worth the extra cost over the LUNA 4?

The LUNA 4 plus adds near-infrared light and firming-focused features to the LUNA 4 cleanse-and-massage design, and it is larger and heavier. If you only want cleansing and massage, the standard LUNA 4 covers that at a lower price.

Can I use any cleanser with a LUNA device?

Use a gentle, non-gritty cleanser. Avoid scrubs, clay or silicone-based cleansers that the brand may advise against for silicone devices, and check the manual for the specific model.

Definitions

Lace front wig

A lace front wig has a sheer lace panel along the hairline where individual strands are hand-tied. This is designed to create a more natural-looking part and edge when styled away from the face.

Closure wig

A closure wig uses a smaller lace piece, often 4x4 or 5x5 inches, placed at the top of the head to allow limited parting space. It is typically paired with wefted hair underneath.

360 lace wig

A 360 lace wig has lace extending around the entire perimeter of the head, which manufacturers commonly market as allowing high ponytails or updos with visible lace all around.

Glueless wig

A glueless wig is constructed with combs, adjustable straps, or an elastic band so it can be worn without adhesive. This is often marketed toward people who want a quicker install for short-term wear, such as a Halloween night.

Bundles and closures

Hair bundles are wefted strands sold by length and texture, usually sewn into a style and finished with a closure or frontal piece to complete the hairline.

Flash sale / clearance sale

These are retailer-labeled promotional terms indicating a temporary price reduction or inventory clearance. The labels describe marketing status, not product quality, so specifications still need independent verification.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming "sale" means better quality: Promotional labels describe pricing and timing, not product grade. Always read the full specification list rather than relying on the sale badge.
  • Skipping lace type research: Buying a narrow closure when you planned a high ponytail can limit your styling options. Match lace width to your intended hairstyle first.
  • Ignoring density numbers: A lower density than expected can look thinner than imagined for a dramatic costume. Compare the density percentage to reference photos if available.
  • Choosing human hair without a styling plan: Human hair needs more aftercare than some buyers expect, including gentle washing and heat protection. Decide whether you have time for that upkeep before choosing it over a simpler option.
  • Underestimating install time: Even glueless wigs can take longer than expected for first-time wearers. Practice before the actual event if possible.
  • Not checking shipping windows: Seasonal sale periods can affect processing and delivery timing. Confirm estimated shipping dates against your event date before ordering.
  • Overlooking return policies: Clearance or flash-sale items sometimes carry different return terms. Read the policy before purchase, not after an issue arises.
  • Buying length or texture based on photos alone: Listing photos can represent styled hair that differs from the natural texture. Cross-check the written description for texture and length details.

Is human hair or synthetic better for a one-time costume?

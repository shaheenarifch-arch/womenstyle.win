Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: October 12, 2026

WomenStyle (womenstyle.win) is an independent shopping guide covering women's fashion, beauty, hair, footwear and jewelry. We are reader-supported: some links on this site are affiliate links, and we may earn a commission when you click one and make a qualifying purchase. This comes at no additional cost to you.

How affiliate links work here

Many of the offers and product images on WomenStyle are supplied through affiliate networks, including Awin. When you select a sponsored link, the network places a tracking cookie or similar identifier so the retailer can recognise that you arrived from WomenStyle. If you buy within the retailer's referral window, we may receive a small percentage of the sale.

Sponsored links on this site are marked in the page code as sponsored and open the retailer's own website in a new tab.

What this means for you

  • You pay the same price whether or not you use our links.
  • All orders, payments, shipping, returns, warranties and customer service are handled by the retailer, not by WomenStyle.
  • Prices, discounts, codes and stock levels shown in partner banners are controlled by the retailer and can change at any time. Always confirm the current details on the retailer's website before you buy.

Our editorial approach

Commissions help keep WomenStyle running, but they do not determine what we feature. Being an affiliate partner does not mean we have personally tested every product, and a featured offer is not a guarantee of quality, fit or results. Please read product descriptions, sizing guides and reviews on the retailer's site and choose what is right for you.

FTC compliance

This disclosure is provided in line with the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's Guides Concerning the Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising (16 CFR Part 255).

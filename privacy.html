Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit WomenStyle (womenstyle.win), how it is used, and the choices you have. We aim to collect as little as possible.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. If you email or call us, we receive your name, contact details and anything you choose to include in your message. We use this only to reply to you.
  • Technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and the time of your visit. This is used to deliver the site securely and reliably.

WomenStyle does not offer user accounts, does not sell products directly and does not take payments, so we never collect card or payment details.

Affiliate links and cookies

When you click a sponsored link, you leave WomenStyle and go to a retailer's website through an affiliate network such as Awin. The network and the retailer may set cookies or use similar technologies to record that the visit came from WomenStyle and to attribute any resulting purchase. Product images in our carousels are also loaded from affiliate network servers, which may receive standard technical information from your browser when the images load.

These third parties process data under their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them. You can learn more in Awin's privacy notice.

How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and secure the website.
  • To respond to your questions and messages.
  • To understand which pages and offers are useful so we can improve the site.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Your choices

  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies will not stop you from using WomenStyle, though retailers may not be able to attribute your visit.
  • Depending on where you live (for example California, Virginia, the UK or the EU), you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at agents@getservices.ai.

Children's privacy

WomenStyle is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

External websites

Our pages link to retailer and social media websites that we do not control. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to agents@getservices.ai.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about how WomenStyle works, prices and promo codes, shipping and returns, and how to reach us.

What is WomenStyle?

WomenStyle is an independent shopping guide for women’s fashion, beauty, hair, shoes and jewelry. We highlight products and offers from online retailers and link you to their websites to buy.

Do you sell products directly?

No. WomenStyle doesn’t sell, ship or take payment for anything. When you click a product, you go to the retailer’s website, and your order is placed with them.

How does WomenStyle make money?

Some links are affiliate links. If you buy through one, the retailer may pay us a small commission. You pay the same price either way. See our affiliate disclosure.

Are the prices and discounts on WomenStyle up to date?

Prices, promo codes and stock are set by each retailer and can change at any time. Always check the final price, including shipping and tax, on the retailer’s checkout page.

Who handles shipping, returns and refunds?

The retailer you buy from handles delivery, returns, exchanges, refunds and warranties under its own policies. Check the return window and who pays return shipping before ordering.

Why didn’t a promo code work?

Codes often have conditions such as minimum spend, selected items, new customers only or an end date. The retailer’s page has the current terms. Contact the retailer if a valid code isn’t applying.

How do I choose the right size?

Measure yourself and compare with the retailer’s own size chart, since sizes vary between brands. Our sizing guide walks through it step by step.

Is it safe to buy through your links?

Our links take you directly to the retailer’s own website, where checkout is handled securely by them. Check that the address bar shows the retailer’s site before entering payment details.

Do you collect my personal information?

We don’t have accounts or checkout, so we don’t collect payment details. Retailers and affiliate networks may use cookies when you click through. See our privacy policy.

Can I suggest a product or brand?

Yes. Email us at agents@getservices.ai with the product or store you’d like to see featured.

How can I contact WomenStyle?

Email agents@getservices.ai or call 313-729-1534. For questions about an order, contact the retailer directly, since they hold your order details.

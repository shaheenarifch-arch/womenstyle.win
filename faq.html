Q&A

Frequently asked questions

Is WomenStyle an online store?

No. WomenStyle is an independent shopping and editorial guide. Product links lead to third-party merchant websites.

Does WomenStyle earn commissions?

Some links may be affiliate links. WomenStyle may earn a commission from qualifying purchases, normally at no additional cost to the shopper.

Who handles orders and returns?

The merchant shown on the destination website handles checkout, payment, delivery, returns, warranties and customer service under its own policies.

Are prices and products guaranteed?

No. Merchants control their own prices, availability and product information. Confirm current details on the merchant website.

How are products selected?

WomenStyle organizes products by category usefulness and shopping intent. Inclusion does not replace the reader’s own research.

Buying guides

Choose with more confidence

Apparel checklist

Check the merchant’s size chart, fabric composition, care instructions, garment measurements and return window. Compare garment measurements with clothing you already own.

Hair-tool checklist

Confirm voltage, plug type, heat settings, attachments, safety features, warranty and suitability for your hair type. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions.

Footwear checklist

Review the merchant’s sizing chart, heel height, materials, width guidance, closure type and return policy. Consider the intended occasion and expected wear time.

Skincare checklist

Read the complete ingredient list, directions and warnings on the merchant or manufacturer page. Patch testing and professional advice may be appropriate for individual sensitivities.

Body-jewelry checklist

Verify gauge, length, diameter, closure and material. For new or healing piercings, seek guidance from a qualified piercer and follow appropriate aftercare advice.

These guides provide general shopping information, not medical or professional advice.
